What the app does to your Mac

  • With the Accessibility permission you grant, it reads the titles and positions of your windows and moves, resizes, minimizes and restores them. It closes nothing and launches nothing.
  • When a saved layout needs more Mission Control desktops than exist, it adds them by opening Mission Control and pressing its "+" button.
  • Moving windows between desktops uses macOS interfaces that Apple does not document. A macOS update can stop that part working; the app then still restores displays, positions and sizes.
  • Updating a profile replaces its saved layout. There is no undo.
